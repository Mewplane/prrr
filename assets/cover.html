FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about PRRR Vision. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our support team.

What cameras are compatible with PRRR Vision?

PRRR Vision supports any camera that uses ONVIF Profile S or T protocols, as well as cameras with RTSP streaming capabilities. This includes models from major manufacturers such as Hikvision, Dahua, Axis, Hanwha, Vivotek, and Reolink. Our compatibility database covers over 3,000 camera models. If your camera supports RTSP streaming, it will likely work with our platform.

How does the AI motion detection reduce false alarms?

Traditional motion detection triggers on any pixel change, including shadows, leaves, rain, and lighting variations. Our AI model analyzes the video feed to identify and classify objects before generating alerts. You can configure rules like "alert only on person detected in zone A after business hours" or "ignore vehicles in parking area during daytime." This dramatically reduces the noise from conventional motion detection and ensures your security team focuses on genuine events.

Can I keep recordings on my own servers instead of the cloud?

Yes. Our Enterprise plan supports hybrid and fully on-premises deployments. You can run the PRRR Vision management server on your own hardware while optionally syncing metadata and alerts to the cloud. This is popular with organizations that have strict data residency requirements or existing storage infrastructure. Contact our sales team for architecture guidance on on-premises deployments.

What bandwidth does the system require?

Bandwidth requirements depend on the number of cameras and resolution settings. A typical 1080p camera at 15 fps uses approximately 2-4 Mbps of upload bandwidth for cloud recording. Our smart codec selection can reduce this by up to 60% using H.265 compression and event-based recording (only full-quality during motion events). For a 16-camera deployment at 1080p, we recommend at least 25 Mbps upload bandwidth with cloud recording enabled.

Is my video footage encrypted?

Absolutely. All video data is encrypted with AES-256 both in transit and at rest. Camera streams are secured with TLS 1.3 from the camera to our cloud platform. Cloud storage uses server-side encryption with customer-managed keys available on Enterprise plans. We never access your footage unless explicitly authorized for technical support purposes. Our infrastructure is SOC 2 Type II certified and undergoes annual third-party security audits.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial of our Professional plan with full access to all features including AI analytics, cloud recording, and multi-site management. No credit card is required to start. At the end of the trial, you can choose a plan that fits your needs or simply let the trial expire. We will never charge you without explicit consent. If you need more time to evaluate, our sales team can extend your trial period upon request.
